Photography that moves between worlds

Still Sonic Media is a photography project rooted in honest observation — street scenes, portraiture, travel, and the moments that fall between categories.

From the dusty roads of Tunisia to the neon quiet of festival venues, the work follows a simple instinct: point the camera at what feels true.
